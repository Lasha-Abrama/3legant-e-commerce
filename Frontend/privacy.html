Privacy Policy

Effective September 1, 2026

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide when you create an account, place an order, update your profile, join our newsletter, submit a review, or contact us. This may include your name, email address, phone number, billing and shipping addresses, account credentials, order details, and messages.

We also receive limited technical information needed to operate and secure the store, such as request timestamps and IP-derived security signals. Your browser stores the access token used to keep you signed in and the contents of your shopping cart.

2. How we use information

We use your information to provide and improve the store, authenticate your account, process and deliver orders, manage payments and refunds, maintain wishlists and reviews, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, and send newsletters you request.

3. Payments and service providers

Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not receive or store your full card number. We use service providers, including database and image-hosting providers, only as needed to operate the store. Those providers process information under their own privacy terms and security practices.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us host the store, process payments, deliver orders, or prevent fraud. We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect customers, the store, or the public.

5. Retention and security

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain transaction and security records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We use technical and organizational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices

You can update account and address information from your account page. You may unsubscribe from promotional emails using the instructions in the message or contact us for assistance. You may also ask about access, correction, or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal and operational requirements.

7. Children

The store is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Policy updates

We may update this policy as the store or applicable requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date.

9. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email hello@loamandco.com or use our contact form.
